The audit is a 7-question quiz that finds a person's biggest Claude "token tax", then asks them to save a seat for the webinar "Tokenmaxxing on Claude". In five days it produced 217 completed audits and 80 people tapped the seat link. Two pushes did almost all of it: an untagged link on the afternoon of 23 Oct, and the WhatsApp posts on the evening of 24 Oct, which were tagged and converted best. The homepage embed adds views but very few starts.

What happened so far

20 Oct
Page built for Instagram-ad traffic: one question per screen, a story-style progress bar, and a pinned CTA. That evening the result screen became animated, with each leak drawn and flipped to its fix. A leak list, "The deal" card and an optional use-case form were added after the result. The CTA was pointed at the class registration link.
21 Oct
Restyled to match 100xengineers.com: Space Grotesk, coral #FE6242, light theme only.
22 Oct
Analytics went live. PostHog now records every screen reached, alongside the answers posted to the Google Sheet.
23 Oct, morning
Embedded on the 100xengineers.com homepage below the hero (tagged utm_source=website). Q1 got a "Not Claude yet" option.
23 Oct, 4 PM to 7 PM IST
Spike one. 95 people started the quiz in three hours, 67 of them between 4 and 5 PM. They came on an untagged direct link, two thirds on mobile. This window holds 45% of all starts.
23 Oct, evening
Tracking can now tell the homepage embed from a direct link, and logs when the box is actually seen on screen. The use-case form now says what's missing and offers one-tap starter answers.
24 Oct, daytime
Quiet: 8 starts by 3 PM, mostly from the homepage.
24 Oct, 7 PM to 1 AM IST
Spike two, tagged this time. WhatsApp links with utm_source=WTS and WTSChannel brought 66 starts, 32 of them in the 8 PM hour, almost all on mobile. This window holds 31% of all starts, and 6 of the 8 real use cases.
25 Oct
13 starts by 10 AM, 9 of them from WhatsApp links still being opened. The webinar is at 3 PM today.

Insights for us

Marked Changed where the 24 Oct version said something different. Nothing flipped between 01:00 and 10:00 on 25 Oct, only the numbers moved.

1

Tagged WhatsApp links worked, and they converted best. Changed

On 24 Oct the links went out with UTMs (WTS and WTSChannel), as the last report asked. They brought 66 starts in one evening and 73 by this morning, a third of all starts. 56% to 63% of the people who reached a result tapped "Save my seat", against 41% to 43% for the untagged 23 Oct link and the homepage. The 23 Oct source is still unknown.

DoUse WhatsApp first for the next webinar push, and keep one UTM per group or channel. Still find what went out around 4 PM on 23 Oct, since it was the biggest single source.
2

The homepage embed is still a weak entry point.

591 homepage loads gave 19 starts. Counting only people who had the box on screen, 7% started (11 of 169). Homepage visitors aren't there for a quiz, and a small iframe has to win them over in one glance.

DoSwap the iframe for a slim banner ("Find your Claude token leak in 60 seconds") that opens the full page, or drop it after the webinar if it stays under 10%.
3

The quiz itself is not the problem. The result converts, and better than before. Changed

79% of starters reach the result, and 48% of them tap "Save my seat" (24 Oct: 76% and 43%). Most people who leave do so after Q1, before they're invested.

DoKeep the flow as it is. Compare the registrations on class.100xengineers.com with the 80 taps to see how many people we lose on the registration page.
4

The "custom token-spend plan" offer has started to land, but we are still short of 10. Changed

8 real use cases came in (24 Oct: 1), 7 of them since the WhatsApp posts. 24 people tapped "Put me up for the 10", so the intent is there: 6 were stopped by a missing field, and the rest left the form. 4 of the 8 used the one-tap starters word for word, so the starters are helping. The use cases: summarising long PDFs and docs (3), research across web pages (2), debugging from logs and test output, reading screenshots, parsing docs and sheets to write software specs, coding and debugging, and management presentations.

DoPick from the 8 now, and fill the last 2 in the webinar ("drop your use case in chat"). PDFs and docs is the clear theme for the custom plans.
5

Nearly everyone gets the same answer, more so with the new traffic.

77% are told Raw-format is their biggest leak, and 84% of the newest arrivals. That is true to the data (78% feed PDFs, 72% screenshots), but the result can feel generic, and people comparing results in a WhatsApp group will notice. It also hides Cache break, which 82% have.

DoAfter the webinar, re-weight the scoring or show the top two leaks. Today, the people in the room will mostly have the Raw-format result.
6

What the audience tells us for today's session. Changed

  • Limits are the pain. 75% hit a usage limit every week. Frame each fix as "more messages before the limit", not as tokens saved.
  • Free is now the biggest single plan. 41% Free, 39% Pro. 59% still pay, and 41% default to Opus, so "right model for the job" still lands, but show what a Free user can do too.
  • Lean App, then Code. 56% are on the App track (24 Oct: half and half). Open each demo in the Claude app, then show the Claude Code version.
  • Lead with: PDFs and raw files, screenshots, then cache breaks from switching models (82% switch at least sometimes).
  • Keep connectors/MCP short. 37% use none, and only 39% have the Startup tax.
