Module 1

Where the money goes

Every time you send a message, Claude reads the whole chat again from the top. Press play and watch the pile of old messages grow.

Show the maths

Each message pays: old history × cache-read price, the last exchange × cache-write price, your new message × input price, Claude's answer × output price. Words to tokens at 4 ÷ 3 (estimate).

Module 3

Screenshot tax

Claude cuts every image into 28-pixel squares and charges for each square. Crop to the part that matters and watch the count fall.

Check with Claude

Uses your own Anthropic API key. The cropped image goes from your browser to Anthropic, not to us.

Show the maths

Tokens = ceil(width ÷ 28) × ceil(height ÷ 28), with the long edge capped at 2,576 px (Anthropic docs).

Drag the corners to crop. Drag inside to move. Paste or drop any image.

Module 5

      
The restart fee

      
Think of a shop that keeps your usual order ready. Come back soon with the same order and it's quick and cheap. Change the order, or stay away too long, and they start from scratch and charge for all of it.

      

        

          
          
          
          
          
        

        

      

      
Show the maths
The saved copy is called the cache. A normal message re-reads it at the cache-read price. Switching model, adding a tool, or waiting longer than the cache lifetime throws the copy away, and that message writes the whole history again at the cache-write price. The lifetime is 5 minutes on API keys and 1 hour on paid plans (Anthropic docs).
