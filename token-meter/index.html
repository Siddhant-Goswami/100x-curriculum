Module 1

Where the money goes

Every turn, Claude re-reads the whole chat from its cache. Drag the chat longer and watch what you pay for.

Each turn, one column. Turn 1 writes the starting context and runs off the top.

Turn n pays: history × cache-read price, the last exchange × cache-write price, your new message × input price, Claude's answer × output price. Words to tokens at 4 ÷ 3 .

Module 2

The Caveman myth

"Tell Claude to answer like a caveman and save tokens." Shrink every answer and see what the bill does.

Same chat and maths as module 1. Only output tokens shrink, and so does what later turns re-read.

Module 3

Screenshot tax

Claude cuts every image into 28 px squares and charges for each one. Crop and shrink to see the count fall.

Drag the corners to crop. Drag inside to move the crop.

Uses your own Anthropic API key. The cropped image goes from your browser to Anthropic, not to us.

Tokens = ceil(width ÷ 28) × ceil(height ÷ 28), with the long edge capped at 2,576 px .

Module 5

Cache breaker

A chat with 100K tokens of history. Step through 10 turns. Some moves throw the cache away, and the next turn writes it all again.

A normal turn reads the history at the cache-read price. After a break it pays the cache-write price on all of it. The cache lives 5 minutes on API keys and 1 hour on paid plans .
