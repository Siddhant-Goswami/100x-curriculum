Privacy

This site holds course pages for 100xEngineers learners. Here is what it keeps when you use it.

Signing in with Google

The AI Delegation Framework page asks you to sign in with Google before it checks your tasks with Jev, a paid AI model. Signing in lets us give every learner a fair share of checks each day.

  • From Google we use only your account's ID number, and only to tell learners apart. We turn it into a one-way code and keep that code. We don't keep your name, email address or photo.
  • A cookie on this site keeps you signed in for 14 days. Signing out or clearing cookies removes it.
  • Against that code we keep counts of how many checks you used, so the daily limit works.

Your tasks

  • The tasks you type are sent to Jev, from TypeSafe, to be sorted. TypeSafe doesn't train on them.
  • We keep Jev's answer for 30 days under a one-way code of the task, so the same task isn't checked twice. We don't keep the text you typed.
  • Your task list stays in your own browser, in local storage.

Analytics

We count page visits and button clicks with PostHog to see which pages help. These events don't include what you type.

Questions or removal

Write to goswami.siddh@gmail.com and we will answer, or remove anything kept about you.
