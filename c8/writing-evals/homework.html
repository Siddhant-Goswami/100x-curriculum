Homework · Writing Evals · Cohort 8

Build the real eval

The eval from your exit ticket, for the feature you are building. Start from the Promptfoo project the session page exported.

Due one week after the session.Graded with the rubric below, written before any submission was read.

01What to hand in

  1. Thirty inputs, sampled from your logs or written with a domain expert. Name the strata.
  2. A three-layer rubric: hard constraints in code, quality dimensions with a 1 and a 5 written for each, and a preference baseline.
  3. A Promptfoo config that runs in CI with a pass threshold. Hard constraints as javascript assertions, quality as llm-rubric.
  4. Ten items graded by two people, independently. Report expert-vs-expert agreement, judge-vs-expert agreement, and one rubric edit that moved the second number.

The exported zip has an agreement.py that prints both numbers from Promptfoo's results. Replace its labels with your two people's.

02How it is graded

CriterionWeightFails if
Decision statement15%No action changes with the score
Input sampling20%Inputs are author-invented with no stratification
Hard constraints in code20%Any constraint is checked by the judge instead of code
Judge calibration30%No human labels, or agreement reported without the expert-vs-expert baseline
Rubric iteration15%No before-and-after edit with its agreement delta

The signal we look for: you report your judge's agreement number without being asked, and you treat a 60% judge as unshippable rather than as a result.

03About the labels in the session

The session's items were built for teaching: each one is assembled from parts whose quality is known. The two "expert" passes are that known quality plus independent noise, set so the two passes agree about as often as two domain experts do. They stand in for experts so the session can run in twenty minutes. Your homework uses two real people.
