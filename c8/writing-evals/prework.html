Read one post, run one command, bring one feature. The session starts from all three.

Sent three days before the session, first week of November 2026.About 20 minutes.

GDPval: measuring the performance of our models on real-world tasks.

Come with one sentence: what did they spend the most effort on?

02Run Promptfoo once

You need Node 20 or newer. In an empty folder:

npx promptfoo@latest init
npx promptfoo@latest eval

If it prints a results table, you are done. You will not use it in the room; the homework starts from a Promptfoo project the session page exports for you.

03Bring one feature
