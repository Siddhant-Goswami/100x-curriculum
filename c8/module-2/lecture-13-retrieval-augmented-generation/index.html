Retrieval-augmented generation

Five questions about our own notes, and what each one hands you.

The lecture in one line

RAG is a tool call in three steps: retrieve, augment, generate. When the data is too big or too costly to paste, cut it into chunks, label them so you can filter, and send only the few that matter.
