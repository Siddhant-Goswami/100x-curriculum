Your brain

A folder your tool reads before it answers you. Start it tonight, then use it for a week.

20 minutes in class, then about 10 minutes a day. Finish it before Lecture 17, Fri 4 Dec.

The brief

Start your own brain from the template. Give it your North Star, one source and one fact. Write three questions whose answers you know, and score them. Use it for one job for a week (keeping you up to date for your North Star), fix one thing it got wrong, and on Fri 4 Dec ask for the fact again.

Get the template

No GitHub account? Download the same folder as a zip.

01Why

  • Lecture 6: the LLM can think and cannot remember. One file, sent back with the next prompt, fixed that for Aarav.
  • Lecture 14: search finds the few passages that matter, when they were written.
  • Tonight: the 100x brain knows the course. It knows nothing about you. Yours does.

06Check it and share it

Run python3 tools/check.py in the folder, or ask your tool to run it. It ticks off what is done.

Before Lecture 17, Fri 4 Dec, submit on the LMS: a link to your brain or a zip of the folder, and the screenshot.

07If your tool will not open a folder

Do it with one file. Write me.md, and paste it at the top of every new chat. Tell it one fact, add the fact to the file yourself, and ask again next week. That is memory.txt from Lecture 6, and it still counts.

08If you want more

  • Ask the course: the template already holds the 100x brain's address. Ask your brain a course question and see where it goes.
  • Code track: give your brain its own address with mcp_server.py from Lecture 12, and search it with rag.py from Lecture 14.
  • Anyone: connect one more source you already own, such as your Google Sheet from Lecture 11.

Keep to one job this week. Five sources in week one is a pile, not a brain.
