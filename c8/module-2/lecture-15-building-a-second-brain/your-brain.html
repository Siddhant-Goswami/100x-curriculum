Your brain

A folder an LLM reads before it answers you. Start it tonight, hand it in this weekend, then keep using it.

20 minutes in class tonight, 15 after class, 45 after Lecture 16. Hand it in by Sun 29 Nov.

The brief

You need a text editor and any chat with an LLM. Nothing else. Start your own brain from the template: you carry the files to the LLM and back. Give it your North Star, one fact and one thing you saved. Write three questions whose answers you know, and score them. After Lecture 16, fill your card on it and hand it in by Sun 29 Nov. Then use it for one job for a week (keeping you up to date for your North Star), and on Fri 4 Dec ask for the fact again.
