LLM memory: building a second brain

Last week you built search. Tonight you meet a question search cannot answer, and decide what to do about it.

Bring tonight

Your laptop, the tool you open folders with (Antigravity, Claude Code, Codex or Cursor), your North Star from the workshop, and one thing you saved this week and have not read yet (an article, a post, a newsletter), saved as a text file. No code needed.

01

The questions

  1. In Lecture 6 the app forgot Monday by Tuesday. Last week you built search. What can search still not find?
  2. One question, three lectures. How many of the top three passages hold the answer?
  3. You wrote one page. There are over a hundred. Who writes the rest, and how would you know it is right?
  4. Before it answers: how many pages will it open?
  5. This answer is wrong, and the LLM did nothing wrong. What did?
  6. What should it know about you that the course notes never will?

02

The 100x brain

Everything Cohort 8 has been taught, as one folder of plain files. It holds 126 course pages as they were published, and 126 short pages an LLM wrote from them: one per lecture, one per idea, and a few that run across lectures. Every line links to the place it came from, and a person approves each change.

Open the 100x brain on GitHub, start at index.md and click through.

To ask it from your own chat app, give the app this address, the way you gave it the sheet's server in Lecture 11:

https://100x-curriculum.vercel.app/api/brain
