Your harness card

Draw what surrounds the model in a system you built. Break it once. Add one box that catches the failure.

On your own, about 45 minutes. Finish it before Lecture 17, Fri 4 Dec.

Done equals

One drawing, five lines, one failure you reproduced, and the same three questions scored before and after you added a box.

01Pick one system

Anything you built in this module that has a model inside it: your Lecture 8 app, your hackathon build, your tool call from Lecture 12, your search from Lecture 14, or your second brain from Lecture 15. One is enough.

02Draw it

Draw every box between the question going in and the answer coming out. Paper is fine. Give each box one of three colours.

  • Follows rules: your code, a search, a database, a tool
  • Predicts: the model
  • A person: you, your user, a reviewer

This is the system on the Harness Bench, drawn the same way.

03Fill five lines

One line each. "Nothing" is an honest answer, and usually the most useful one.

HARNESS CARD System: Model: Reads What does the model read on every call? Asks What can it ask your server to do? Remembers What does the system keep between calls, and where? Checks What checks the answer before it leaves? A rule, judgment, or nothing? Record Where can you see what crossed, afterwards? Three questions with known answers: 1. 2. 3. Before: of 3 right. The failure: Box added: After: of 3 right.

04Break it once

  • Write three questions whose right answers you already know. Make one of them something your system should refuse or cannot know.
  • Run all three. Write down how many came back right.
  • Keep one answer that was not right and still reached the user. Paste it on the card.

05Add one box

Look at your five lines. The failure usually sits on the line where you wrote "nothing". Add one box there, draw it on your picture in its colour, and run the same three questions again.

If the score does not move, say so on the card. A box that does not move the score has not earned its place yet.

06Share it

Share the drawing and the filled card. We go through them in the office hour.
