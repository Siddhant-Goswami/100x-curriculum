Your harness card

Draw what surrounds the model in your brain. Find one answer it got wrong. Add one box that catches it.

On your own, about 45 minutes. It goes in with your brain, by Sun 29 Nov.

Done equals

One drawing, five lines, one wrong answer you kept, and your three questions scored before and after you added a box.

01Your system

The brain you started in Lecture 15. This card is step 03 of Your brain, so you hand in one thing.

Your brain has one job this week, written in now.md. If you have not picked one, try this: keep me up to date for my North Star. Things you save go in, and it tells you which are worth your time.

No brain yet? Use any system you built that has a model inside it.

02Draw it

Draw every box between the question going in and the answer coming out. Paper is fine. Give each box one of three colours.

  • Follows rules: your code, a search, a database, a tool
  • Predicts: the model
  • A person: you, your user, a reviewer

This is the system on the Harness Bench, drawn the same way.

03Fill five lines

One line each. In your brain, most of the answers are files you can open. If you carried the files by hand in Lecture 15, you did Reads and Asks yourself: write that. "Nothing" is an honest answer, and usually the most useful one.

04Find one it got wrong

  • Open eval/questions.md. Your three questions and their marks from Lecture 15 are the "before".
  • Open log.md. Any day you wrote "wrong" is a failure you already caught.
  • Keep one wrong answer. Write it on the card.

All three right, and nothing wrong in the log? Then the questions are too easy for the job. If the job is keeping you up to date, give it a link you know is noise for your North Star and ask whether it is worth your time. You are the only one who knows the right answer, which is the Verifier's Law from Lecture 1.

05Add one box

Look at the wrong answer, then at your five lines. The cause usually sits on a line where the answer was thin: it never read your North Star, it kept a line that was out of date, or nothing checked the answer.

Add one box there. Often it is one rule in AGENTS.md. Draw it on your picture in its colour.

Score the same three questions again and fill the last column in eval/questions.md. If the score does not move, say so on the card. A box that does not move the score has not earned its place yet.

06Hand it in with your brain

Put the drawing and the filled card in your brain's folder. It goes in with the rest of Your brain, on the LMS, by Sun 29 Nov.

07If you want more

If your brain's job is keeping you up to date, steps 05 to 08 of the Harness Engineering 2.0 tutorial do the same thing at full size: label a month of saved links yourself, let a model label them blind, compare, and write one rule. It needs a terminal.
