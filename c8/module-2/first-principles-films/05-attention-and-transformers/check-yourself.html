5.6Check yourself

Answer each one in your head, then open it.

What is the difference between a token's code and its embedding?

The code is an agreed or counted number that says nothing about meaning. The embedding is a list of numbers found by training.

Why do "Paris" and "London" drift together if nobody rates the words?

When they sit close, one sentence teaches both, which lowers the loss. Lower loss is the only direction nudges push.

What problem does "bank" expose, and what solves it?

A token gets its spot alone, before seeing its neighbours. Attention lets it move based on the words around it.

Why can a token not look at the tokens after it?

They are the answer it is being trained to guess.

Why does doubling a text roughly quadruple the listening?

Every token listens to every earlier one, so the work grows with the number of pairs.

Words you now own
Embedding
A token's list of numbers: its spot on the map of meaning.

A list of numbers.
Attention
Each token matches its question against earlier tokens' labels and blends their messages.

In your build

  • The model can only listen to what is in the prompt. If "capital" and "France" are not there, nothing can attend to them.
  • Long prompts cost more than their length suggests.
  • Embeddings are what search by meaning runs on. You will use them in Lecture 12.
