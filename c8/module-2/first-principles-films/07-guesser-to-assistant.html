How does a guesser become an assistant?

All that money buys a machine that guesses the next token and nothing more. Yet.

How to play: click the raised pieces in each drawing, or the buttons under it. Reach the goal and the explanation opens.

01A guesser, not an assistant

Everything so far has a name: pre-training. Nobody graded it. The text graded itself.

What you just found

On the web, a question is often followed by another question, as on a quiz page. It guesses well. It does not answer.

Picture a student who has only ever self-studied. The fastest learners self-study most of the time and take a teacher at intervals.

02When nobody can write the answer

First teacher: people write a few tens of thousands of instruction and ideal-response pairs, and the same loop runs on them. The dials barely move. They learn the shape: a question, then an answer, then stop. That is supervised fine-tuning.

But an obedient model answers anything. We want it helpful, honest and safe, and nobody can write that as an answer key.

What you just found

A second, smaller model, trained to predict which answer people pick, is the judge. The big model writes an answer, the judge scores it, and the score is the feedback. That is reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).

03Same loop, different teachers

Three stages, one card.

What you just found

The goal, the loss and the loop never change. Only the feedback slot does. That recipe, in November 2022, produced ChatGPT.

04What can be checked

Take two tasks. Solve 2x + 6 = 14: put x = 4 back in and it checks itself, a million times, for free. Write a good email: good to whom? Every attempt needs a person to read it, and two people disagree.

What you just found

In competition maths, where every answer checks automatically, models went from struggling with school contests to Olympiad gold in about two years. In open-ended writing, progress is far slower. The difference is not the topic. It is whether the mistake can be measured.

Principle NineA machine learns what you can check. The easier the check, the faster it learns.

05Write the check yourself

You ask a model for "a good email" and get something flat. You handed it a goal and no measure. Raise the check yourself.

What you just found

That written rubric is an eval. Collect your ten best emails with what each was replying to, write down what good looks like, and now the output can be scored. You wrote one in Practice set 2.

07Check yourself

Answer in your head, then open it.

Fine-tuning uses tiny data compared with pre-training. What does it teach, if not facts?

The shape of answering: a question comes, then an answer, then stop.

What problem does RLHF solve that written answers cannot?

Qualities like helpful, honest and safe cannot be written as one right answer, but people can pick the better of two.

Which slot on the card changes between the three stages?

Feedback: the text itself, then a person's written answer, then a judge's score.

You want better marketing copy from a model. What do you build first?

An eval: your best examples and a written rubric for what good looks like.
