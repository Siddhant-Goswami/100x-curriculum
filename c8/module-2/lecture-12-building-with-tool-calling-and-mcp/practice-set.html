Give your row a tool

Due before Lecture 13, Fri 20 Nov 2026.

The brief

Do the two steps from the lecture for your own row. Start from the c8-tools repo.

01Pick your tool

Open your Practice set 1 sheet at the row you tested in step 04. Beside it you wrote what the model was missing. Your tool fetches that: from a CSV file, a Supabase table, or an API you already called in this course.

02Step 1: one tool call

Copy tool_call.py and change three things:

  1. The function. Make it fetch what your row was missing. Run it alone first.
  2. The card. Its name, what it does, and what to pass in.
  3. The question. Your row's question.

Run it. Check that the model asked for your function and that the answer uses its result.

03Step 2: the MCP server

  1. Copy mcp_server.py and put your function in it, under @mcp.tool, with a docstring.
  2. Run it. In a second terminal: fastmcp list http://localhost:8000/mcp, then fastmcp call with your tool's name.

04Share it

  • Your two files.
  • The output of python tool_call.py and of fastmcp call.

Once you're done, share it on the Discord. We go through them in the office hour.
