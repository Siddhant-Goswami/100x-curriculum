Run the five moves

The last four weeks, in order.

MoveWhat you learnedWhat you doYou hand in
1. Be the tool by handDiscoverySit with a real person. Do the job yourself before building.Raw record and their reaction.
2. Name the need, then the toolUI, APIFor each capability finish: "the system has to be able to ___." Remember, react, generate, touch. Then map to frontend, API, model, database.Need list, no tool named first.
3. Draw the boundaryAPI, model callMark what is deterministic and what the model judges. Circle the one place the model must not decide.Hand-drawn diagram.
4. Shape the data, lock itData, auth, deployDraw the schema. Mark the one link that is your evidence. Add login and row-level security. Log in as two users. Prove neither sees the other's rows.Hand-drawn schema, two-user test result, live link.
5. Earn the verdictDiscoveryTwo real people use the deployed tool, one cold. Record what they did, not what they said. Write one surprise.Behavioural evidence and the surprise.

No Move 5, no submission.

Follow ten rules

  1. Sat 17 Oct, 9:00 PM IST to Tue 20 Oct, 9:00 PM IST. Late work does not count.
  2. One track. Lock it and your problem statement before Check-in 1.
  3. Solo. People you test are subjects, not teammates.
  4. Any AI tool to build. The grade is what AI cannot fake: real sessions, your diagrams, your surprise.
  5. Hand-drawn means paper. Photo it, paste it.
  6. Every feature traces to a need you wrote in Move 2.
  7. Deployed and touchable. A screenshot is not an app.
  8. Login and row-level security on every track. Two-user test or no submission.
  9. Real people, real evidence. Fabrication is an automatic fail. An honest disproof beats a polished fake.
  10. Consent. Redact names and account numbers.

Post every check-in

  • All six are mandatory. A missed check-in is graded negative.
  • Open one thread in the codeno-code hackathon check-in forum on Discord. Title it with your name.
  • Post every check-in as a reply in that thread. Never a new thread.
  • Post on time, or up to 1 hour late. After 1 hour it is not recorded.
  • Pick the template below and copy it. The first word must be Green or Yellow, spelled exactly. Posts are counted automatically.
  • Attach one proof with every post: a photo, a screenshot, or a link.
