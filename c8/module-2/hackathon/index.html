The rule book

  

    
We build alone, ship for real and prove it with real people. These nine rules bind every builder on both tracks.

    
    
    
  1. 1.Time
    Work submitted after Tue 20 Oct, 9 PM IST does not count, however good it is.
    2. 
    
  2. 2.One problem
    You pick one problem, lock it by Check-in 1 on Sun 9 AM, and do not switch after.
    3. 
    
  3. 3.Solo
    You build alone, and your testers give feedback but never write, design or build any part.
    4. 
    
  4. 4.AI tools
    You may use any AI tool, because what we grade is what a real person did, and AI cannot fake that.
    5. 
    
  5. 5.Paper first
    Every sketch, need list and table starts on paper, and you photograph it and paste it in.
    6. 
    
  6. 6.Needs
    Every feature in your app traces back to a need you wrote down in Move 2.
    7. 
    
  7. 7.Deployed
    Your app runs at a live link anyone can open, because a screenshot is not an app.
    8. 
    
  8. 8.Real evidence
    Every note, screenshot and session is real, and faked evidence fails the whole submission.
    9. 
    
  9. 9.Consent
    You ask before you record anyone, and you hide names and account numbers in everything you post.
    10.
