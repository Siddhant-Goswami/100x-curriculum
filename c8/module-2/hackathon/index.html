Ten rules

Break one and the submission does not count.

  1. On time. Late work does not count.
  2. One problem. Locked by Check-in 1.
  3. Solo. Testers are not teammates.
  4. Any AI tool. The proof is what AI cannot fake.
  5. Draw on paper. Photo it, paste it.
  6. Features trace to needs. Every one, from Move 2.
  7. Deployed. A screenshot is not an app.
  8. Login + RLS. Two-user test, every problem.
  9. Real evidence only. Faking it fails. Honest beats polished.
  10. Get consent. Hide names and account numbers.

  3. A live link
