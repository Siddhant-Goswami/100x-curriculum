04Rules

Write before you reveal.

Every "Pause and think" box in the lab needs a line from you before the reveal opens. Keep what you wrote even when the reveal disagrees; that gap is the learning.

Pointers on the many side. Always.

One cell cannot hold many. If you ever want to put a list in a cell, you have found a thing that needs its own table.

History is a row, not a cell.

If the product must remember what was true before, the old value cannot be overwritten. Make it a row with a time on it.

No tool yet.

Do not open Supabase this week. The model is a paper exercise on purpose: tables you cannot describe in words you cannot build in any tool.

LLM help: questions yes; the model no.

Ask a model to explain a shape you do not get. Do not paste a brief in and copy tables out. The briefs are short enough that placing the pointers yourself is the learning.

Hold this

You cannot store what you cannot describe. Three questions describe any product: the things, what each has, how they connect. Everything a database does is built on those answers.
