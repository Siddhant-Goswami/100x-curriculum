03Draw Your HLD

A high-level design (HLD) shows every box in an app, whose machine it lives on, and the calls between them. This is Aarav's, from class. Read it, then draw yours.

Your turn

Draw the same picture for your Assignment 1 app (Track B: Aarav's, from memory). Paper or any drawing app.

  • The user: what goes in (input) and what comes out (output)
  • Numbered boxes, nested: machine › program › piece
  • A dashed zone per machine: the user's, yours, or someone else's
  • Every arrow is an API call: its address and action in the box it reaches, and where the key lives
  • Storage: a box on your server with a question mark inside
  • Under each zone: why those boxes live there (too big, private, or holds a key)

Your question mark

What is worth storing?

Not everything that passes through your server. Only what your app needs to answer its questions next time, such as "Aarav's last plan". Store too much and you pay for it. Store too little and the app forgets.

Then: the Modelling Lab

The Modelling Lab turns that question into three: what are the things? what does each have? how do they connect? Answer them for Aarav in sections 1 to 5, then for your app in section 7. Write your tables into the box in place of the question mark.

05Rules

Write before you reveal.

Every "Pause and think" box in the lab needs a line from you before the reveal opens. Keep what you wrote, even when the reveal disagrees. That gap is where you learn.

Your questions decide what is worth storing.

Write your three questions before any table. A column that no question needs is a column you do not store.

Pointers on the many side. Always.

One cell cannot hold many. If you want to put a list in a cell, you have found a thing that needs its own table.

History is a row, not a cell.

If the product must remember what was true before, never overwrite the old value. Make it a row with a time on it.

No tool yet.

Do not open Supabase this week. The model is a paper exercise on purpose: if you cannot describe a table in words, you cannot build it in any tool.

LLM help: questions yes; the model no.

Ask a model to explain a shape you do not get. Do not paste a brief in and copy tables out. Placing the pointers yourself is the point.
