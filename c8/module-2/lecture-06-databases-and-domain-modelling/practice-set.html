01Draw Yours

The same picture for your Assignment 1 app. No app yet? Draw Aarav's from memory. Paper or any drawing app.

  • The user: input in, output out
  • Numbered boxes, nested: machine › program › piece
  • A dashed zone per machine: the user's, yours, or someone else's, and why each box lives there
  • Every arrow is an API call: its address in the box it reaches, and where the key lives
  • Storage: a box on your server with a question mark inside

Your question mark

What is worth storing?

Only what your app needs to answer its questions next time, such as "Aarav's last plan". Store too much and you pay for it. Store too little and the app forgets.

02Fill the Box in the Modelling Lab

The lab splits the question into three: what are the things? what does each have? how do they connect? Answer them for Aarav in sections 1 to 5, then for your app in section 7. Your tables replace the question mark.

04Rules

  • Write a line before every reveal in the lab, and keep it even when the reveal disagrees.
  • Questions before tables. A column no question needs is not worth storing.
  • Pointers go on the many side. A list in a cell means a missing table.
  • History is a row, not a cell.
  • No Supabase this week. Ask an LLM to explain, not to model for you.
