Databases and domain modelling

Your app can think. Now decide what it should remember.

The lecture in one line

Aarav's app can think but cannot remember. Add storage: start with a file, keep it on your server, and store only what your app's questions need.

What is worth storing? The lab answers with three questions.

1

What are the things?Nouns the app must create, find, change and delete on their own. Each gets a table.

2

What does each thing have?The facts you need to recreate one. Each is a column.

3

How do they connect?One-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-many. A pointer on the many side.
