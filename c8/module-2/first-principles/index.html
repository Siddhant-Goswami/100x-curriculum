From sand to language models

What really happens when your app saves a row, or a model answers a question. Built up from a single switch, with no maths needed.

Start with lesson 1.1 About 2.5 hours in all ·

How it works

  1. Read the short opening. Each lesson is one page and one idea.
  2. Play the drawing. Reach its goal and the explanation opens. A few lessons are a short read with no drawing.
  3. Press Next. Contents, at the top left, shows every lesson and which ones you have done.

Nothing to submit. Your progress stays on this device.

What you have found

Each principle shows up here once you finish its chapter.

  1. Atoms make electricity. Electricity flips transistors. Transistors speak in ones and zeros.
  2. Fast memory forgets. Lasting memory lags.
  3. A little data says how to read the rest.
  4. Faster reading means a second copy.
  5. Record the change in one place before changing anything else.
  6. Keep one original. Every copy can be thrown away and remade.
  7. A count only knows what it saw. A pattern can guess what it didn't.
  8. Everything a model writes is the next token, guessed from patterns held in its dials.
  9. Learning is reducing mistakes toward a goal, with feedback from every attempt.
  10. Meaning comes from company. Every token listens to the ones before it, and becomes what it hears.
  11. The maths is cheap. Moving the numbers is the cost.
  12. A machine learns what you can check. The easier the check, the faster it learns.
  13. A machine learns the check you wrote, not the goal you meant.
