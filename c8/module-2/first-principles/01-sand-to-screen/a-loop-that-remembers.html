Memory: a loop that remembers

Wire two gates in a loop, so each one's output feeds the other's gate.

What you just found

Each gate holds the other one open, so the loop stays on with nothing feeding it. That is how a chip keeps one after the input is gone. Billions of these loops are its memory.
