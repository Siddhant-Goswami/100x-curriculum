Chapter 7 · How does a guesser become an assistant? · Lesson 4 of 6

7.4What can be checked

Take two tasks. Solve 2x + 6 = 14: put x = 4 back in and it checks itself, a million times, for free. Write a good email: good to whom? Every attempt needs a person to read it, and two people disagree.

What you just found

In competition maths, where every answer checks automatically, models went from struggling with school contests to Olympiad gold in about two years. In open-ended writing, progress is far slower. The difference is not the topic. It is whether the mistake can be measured.

Principle NineA machine learns what you can check. The easier the check, the faster it learns.
