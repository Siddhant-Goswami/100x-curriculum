Write the eval

Turn the score you gave by feel into a check anyone could run the same way.

On your own, about 45 minutes. Finish it before Lecture 11, Thu 12 Nov.

The brief

Take the task you tested in Practice set 1, section 04 and write its eval: the decision it serves, the inputs, the hard lines, what a 1 and a 5 look like, and who grades. The exercise walks you through it.

Start the exercise

01Why

  • Lecture 9: a model can take over a task as far as its output can be checked.
  • Lecture 10: you tested one task and scored it out of 10, by feel. Someone else could give the same reply a 4 where you gave it a 7.
  • Office hour: a written-down grade is exactly what labs pay experts for, on Mercor and elsewhere.

The eval is that check, written down. From Lecture 11 on, every build ends with "did it get better?", and this is how you will know.

03Share it
