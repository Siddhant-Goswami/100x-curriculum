# Delegation framework: custom tasks sorted by Jev, report, 19 Oct 2026

Built from `custom_tasks_plan.md`. Live at https://100x-curriculum.vercel.app/c8/delegation-framework since 19 Oct 2026.
Commits 752a327 … 06860b4 on `main`, deployed with `vercel --prod`.

## What shipped
- **Page** (`c8/delegation-framework/index.html`): type any job (O*NET and the task bank only add suggestions), write up to 12 tasks with how often and minutes, one Sort call, at most one yes/no question per task, "How you'd check each one" (a rule for amber, up to three criteria for violet, tested by Jev), the story, the table with colour overrides, the K and the agent advice, all built from the learner's tasks. "Copy link" puts the week in the `#w=` hash. If Jev can't be reached, the learner answers the questions by hand.
- **API** (`api/verify.mjs`): `POST /api/verify` sorts; `POST /api/verify?step=check` tests checks, one or `{items:[...]}`. Jev `jev-1.13.0` pinned; thresholds in `T`, mirrored in the page's `decide()`.
- **Data**: `data/bank.json`, 1,500 tasks across 100 roles, built by `build_bank.py` from `bank_written.json`.
- **Eval**: `eval_verify.mjs` with `gold.csv` (300 English tasks: 75 each from software engineers, founders, management consultants, MNC IT services; 25 per bucket per profile) and `checks.csv` (60). `gold_v1_mixed.csv` is the earlier India/Hinglish set, not used now.

## Final thresholds (`T` in api/verify.mjs)
`live .5, reach .2, rule .3, criteria .4, fence 0, top .6, specific .3, isRule .7, isClear .45, fits .6`

## Learnings
1. **Jev reads literally.** "Could an agent get *every* input" sat near 0.5 for most tasks and caused most questions. Rewording to "Are the inputs available on a computer, as files, data, email or apps?" with true/false criteria fixed it.
2. **Ask only what changes the answer.** The first version asked whenever any guardrail was near 0.5: 28% of gold tasks, and 6 of 7 in a live week. Now a question is asked only if a yes and a no would give different buckets, at most one per task, and only when the two option orders disagree or no bucket reaches 0.6. Questions fell to 3% with no loss in accuracy.
3. **Option order rarely matters here.** The two orderings disagreed on only 1.3% of tasks; keep both, since disagreement is the best "unsure" signal.
4. **"Decide…" tasks were the main error.** Authority and judgement calls ("Decide pricing for the enterprise tier") went violet until `live` dropped to 0.5. Software-engineer judgement calls still slip ("Decide whether to rewrite the billing service").
5. **Don't promote criteria to a rule.** Good rubrics score 0.5–0.9 on "is this a rule", so the plan's violet→amber upgrade fired on most of them and sent "Review pull requests" to Autonomous. Removed.
6. **is_clear separates checks cleanly below 0.6.** Vague checks top out at 0.36 and good ones are mostly 0.45–0.75, so 0.45 is the cut.
7. **Jev moves slightly between runs.** The same 300 tasks scored 95.3% from the saved answers and 96.0% live.
8. **Ops.** `TYPESAFE_API_KEY` is a sensitive, Production-only Vercel variable: `vercel env pull` returns it empty. So the eval runs through the live endpoint (`--via`, one call per 40 s to stay under the 20-per-10-minutes limit, about 20 minutes for the full set). It saves Jev's answers to `eval_sigs.json`, and `--replay` re-scores threshold changes offline in seconds.

## Known gaps
- Thresholds were tuned and scored on the same 300 tasks, and a model drafted the labels. Have two people review `gold.csv` and write a held-out set before quoting the score further.
- A reasonable PR-review rubric is rejected as not specific (false negative, safe side). 2 of 15 vague rules still pass as criteria.
- The rate limit is per IP (20 calls in 10 minutes); a class on one network can hit it, and then the page falls back to hand-answered questions.
- English only for now; Hinglish and other languages are not evaluated.
- Unanswered questions are not saved across a reload; the colour is.

## How to re-run
```
node c8/delegation-framework/_plan/eval_verify.mjs --via https://100x-curriculum.vercel.app   # live, ~20 min
node c8/delegation-framework/_plan/eval_verify.mjs --replay                                     # offline, after editing T
```
Change `T` in `api/verify.mjs` and the matching numbers in the page's `decide()` together.
