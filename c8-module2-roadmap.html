The first principle

Input, process, output.

If you can define the right inputs, you can define the outputs you want. The whole job of software engineering, and of AI engineering, is finding the process in between and then building tools so it runs without you. Open the process to see what is inside it.

Input

What comes in. A form, a document, a message, a row of data.

Output

What comes out, and who receives it. Verified by someone who did not build it.

Look inside any process and every step is one of three kinds. The design question for each step is which kind it deserves.

Deterministic

Rule based. Same input, same output, every time. Interfaces, APIs, databases, deployment.

Which steps deserve rules? Weeks 1 to 5 →

Probabilistic

Model based. A prediction, not a guarantee. LLMs, tool calling, retrieval, memory.

Which steps deserve a model? Weeks 5 to 10 →

Human

Judgement. A person stays in the loop wherever a wrong answer costs more than a slow one.

Which steps stay human? Weeks 11 to 12 →
